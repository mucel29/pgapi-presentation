## Findings and acceptance criteria

### A01 — P1 — Put the necessary reasoning into the audience flow

**Repro / observed:** open diffuse, loop, SAH or denoise normally. Definitions/derivations live largely in Detalii; β readouts and glossary are not visible. At hit, select În afară: the inset correctly says MISS while the world ray still touches the sphere, without a short visible statement that this is a separate exercise. The notes already disclose that distinction. **Expected / impact:** the ordinary flow must supply the prerequisites and identify each query; otherwise correct mathematics looks like disconnected assertion.

**Source / evidence:** `src/main.ts:68,72,145,158`; `src/style.css:6,12`; `src/content/chapters.ts:126,1257`; [diffuse](../.build/audit-2026-11-16/experience/05-diffuse.jpg), [independent triangle MISS](../.build/audit-2026-11-16/experience/04-hit-miss.jpg). Raw T01 + E02.

**Redo / acceptance (U):** add a small progressive reasoning block per stop, not every hidden bullet. Define β/PDF before use; show Lambertian `f=ρ/π`, `p=cosθ/π`, hence `β′=βρ`; define SAH symbols beside actual values; expose a small truthful RGB state. Make one inspectable NEE calculation explain area-to-solid-angle PDF, visibility, BSDF/cosine factors and matched MIS weights without turning the slide into a code dump. Label the standalone triangle exercise visibly or give it focused staging. A viewer can explain these points without opening a modal, and the added content fits the tested viewport matrix. Retain detailed notes rather than exposing them wholesale.
